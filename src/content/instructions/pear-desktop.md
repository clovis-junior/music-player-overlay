You need to download **Pear Desktop** (formerly YouTube Music Desktop Application).

[Click here to download Pear Desktop](https://github.com/pear-devs/pear-desktop/releases). It's free!

After downloading it, follow these instructions:

- Open **Pear Desktop**.
- Click on **Plugins** in the top-left menu bar.
- Scroll down the list, find **API Server [Beta]** and toggle it **ON**.
- Hover or click on **API Server [Beta]** to open its sub-menu.
- Go to **Authorization strategy** and select **Authorize on first request**.
  - *Optional:* If you want to bypass prompts completely, you can select **No authorization**.
- Click the button below to connect.
